About

This is a local livestream simulation. Comments are AI-generated fictional voices; there is no broadcast or connected audience. Like and Subscribe only change local button states.

Go live requests your camera and microphone. Speech is processed in your browser; media and comments are never uploaded or saved. End releases both devices and clears chat. Leaving the tab also ends capture.

First use downloads about 680 MB of model assets. Use a WebGPU-capable browser and choose your spoken language before starting.
