About

This is a local livestream simulation. Comments are AI-generated fictional voices; there is no broadcast or connected audience. Viewer counts, likes and subscriptions are fictional reactions to speech. Your Like and Subscribe choices stay separate.

Go live requests your camera and microphone. Speech is processed in your browser; media and comments are never uploaded or saved. End releases both devices and clears chat. Leaving the tab also ends capture.

First use downloads about 1.1 GB of model assets. Use a WebGPU-capable browser and choose your spoken language before starting.
