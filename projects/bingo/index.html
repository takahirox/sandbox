    How to play

    No sign-up, names, or rooms. Everyone here shares the same calls and gets a different card. This browser keeps your card and marks when you reload during a game.

    Mark any called number, including calls made before you joined. The center is free. A row, column, or diagonal earns a verified Bingo, shared with everyone.

    Keep playing after a Bingo. Calls end at 75; the caller can confirm a new game at any time. A new game gives everyone new cards and clears all marks and calls.

    Anyone can claim an open caller role. Only the caller starts games and draws numbers. A disconnected caller has 30 seconds to return before the role opens again.

    This small shared demo supports up to 128 connections and cards per game, with per-device and daily play limits. Disconnected cards are freed on a new game. Clearing browser storage loses your card.

    Start fresh for everyone?

    All cards, marks, Bingo results, and called numbers will be replaced. The new game waits for you to start it.
